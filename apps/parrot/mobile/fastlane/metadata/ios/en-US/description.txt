Parrot is the music player for the music you own. Add your songs and albums from your computer, then stream them to your phone and your computer, wherever you are.

YOUR MUSIC LIBRARY, EVERYWHERE
• Upload your music with the Parrot app for your computer (Windows, Mac, Linux): MP3, FLAC, AAC, ALAC, OGG, Opus and WAV.
• Album covers and song details are filled in for you.
• Browse your albums, artists, songs and playlists, and search your whole library.
• Your library, playlists and liked songs are the same on every device.

LISTEN YOUR WAY
• Create playlists and like songs, on your phone or your computer.
• Sing along with lyrics, synced line by line when available.
• Queue up what plays next, shuffle and repeat.
• Control your music from the lock screen and notifications.

LISTEN OFFLINE
• Download songs, albums or playlists to your phone and listen without internet.
• Songs you add to a downloaded playlist download too.

JUST YOUR MUSIC
• No ads.
• Only you can see your library. Not even we can.
• Parrot doesn't supply music: you listen to the music you add yourself.

FREE TO START
• Create a free SpaceFleet account, with 20 GB of storage included.
• The same account works with Badger, our app for your movies and TV shows.
